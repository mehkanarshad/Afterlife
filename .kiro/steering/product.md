# Product

Afterlife resurrects dead websites by fetching archived snapshots from the Wayback Machine and rendering a modernized version of the original content.

## Purpose

The core experience is rediscovery: users enter a URL, and Afterlife finds the most recent archived version and presents it in a clean, readable form. The content is preserved faithfully — layout quirks, broken images, and 404s from the original are stripped away in favor of the extracted text and structure.

## Tone

Nostalgic but clean. The UI should evoke the feeling of finding an old webpage you forgot existed, without looking dated itself. Use restrained, warm language in copy. Avoid clinical or technical phrasing in user-facing text.

## Non-Goals

- Afterlife is not a full web archiver or crawler.
- It does not store or index pages beyond the current session.
- It does not attempt to recreate original styling or assets.
